Questions

Before you book

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients leave access details with us. If you're home, we work around you.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, we bring everything we need. If your appliances or equipment need a specific cleaner to keep their quality, let us know beforehand so we make no mistakes.

How do you clean long-term stains, oils or build-ups?

We use different chemicals with no side effects to remove stains. For build-ups, we start with our steam machine.

Do you need power at my apartment to use the steam machine?

It works best when power is available at your residence. If you prefer that we do not use your power supply, we bring our own generator at no extra cost.

My toilets and drainage often smell bad. How do you deal with it?

We start with a drainage flushing chemical that clears years of dirt build-up from your drainage. We have products for this, and we also give you techniques to keep the fresh smell lasting.

Do you offer fumigation?

Yes, strictly on request, and you can book it on its own. It is safe. You will need to leave the house for about 3 to 5 hours, and we provide bags to keep your essentials and food safe during the fumigation. The price is quoted on request, depending on what we are tackling.

What if something is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll send the team back to fix it at no charge.

Do you charge for an inspection?

Estimates from your details and photos are free. If you want us to inspect on site, there is a ₦3,000 commitment fee. It is deducted from your total if you book us, and non-refundable if you decide not to go ahead after the visit. Pay the inspection fee here.

Can I change or cancel a visit?

Yes. Give us notice before your visit and we'll reschedule or pause your plan.
